Where your data lives

Eisenhower Matrix for Android · 26 November 2026

Your tasks stay on your phone. The app has no account, no tracking, and no server of ours to send them to. This page explains exactly where your data is, the only ways it can leave your phone, and what I, the maker, can and cannot see.

1. Where it lives

Your tasks, notes, labels, history and settings are saved on your phone, in the app's own private storage. Android keeps that storage closed to other apps. There is no account, no sign-in and no copy anywhere else unless you make one.

2. What leaves your phone, and only when you do it

Way outWhenWhere it goes
Export and shareYou tap Export, Share Today or Share on a taskThe app you pick in Android's share menu, such as email or a messaging app
Backup to a folderYou pick a folder (automatic backups are a Pro feature)That folder on your phone. If the folder syncs to a cloud service, that service gets the file
Backup to a serverYou type in a server addressThat address only. The app has no server of its own and no default address
Android's phone backupAndroid's own backup, if it is on in your phone settingsYour Google account, so your tasks come back on a new phone. Google runs it; I can't see it
Voice typingYou tap the microphoneYour phone's own speech service (usually Google's). Only the words come back; the app never gets the audio and has no microphone permission
Buying ProYou buy or restore ProGoogle Play handles the payment. The app only learns whether you own Pro

A shared task link holds that task's title, quadrant and due date, and opens a simple page on this website. The page loads no tracking or outside scripts.

3. What I can see

Nothing from inside the app. The things that reach me are:

  • emails you choose to send me,
  • reviews you post publicly on Google Play,
  • Google Play's totals, such as the number of installs and crashes,
  • for each Pro purchase, a sales record from Google with an order number and the buyer's country. Never your card details, and never your tasks.

4. Why I can't collect your tasks

  • There is no server of mine in the app. The only internet requests it can make go to a backup address that you type in yourself.
  • There is no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code in the app, and no sign-in.
  • Collecting your tasks would need a new version of the app, and Google Play requires the data label on the store page to say so. Today that label says no data is collected or shared.

5. Why I don't want your data

The app is paid for by a one-time Pro unlock, not by ads or by selling data. Holding other people's to-do lists would only bring me risk and legal duties. Keeping everything on your phone is safer for you and simpler for me.

6. Check it yourself

  • Airplane mode. Turn it on and use the app. Everything works, because nothing needs the internet.
  • The store label. On the Google Play page, open Data safety: it says no data collected and no data shared.
  • Your phone. Android Settings › Apps › Eisenhower Matrix › Permissions lists what the app can use. There is no location, contacts, camera or microphone.

7. Deleting your data

Delete tasks in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything from your phone. Files you exported or backed up are yours to delete where you saved them. A copy in Android's phone backup can be removed in your Google account's backup settings.
