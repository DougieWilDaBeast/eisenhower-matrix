An Eisenhower Matrix widget for Android

17 November 2026

A priority list you have to remember to open is a list you'll forget. Putting it on the home screen means you see today's tasks every time you unlock your phone.

Two widgets, two jobs

Eisenhower Matrix: To-Do List has two free home screen widgets:

  • Today. Shows tasks that are late or due today, so the urgent ones are always in view. Tap a task to open it.
  • Add a task. A bar that opens a new task in one tap, typed or by voice. Say "Call the dentist tomorrow at 3pm" and the title, date and time fill in.

How to add them

  1. Open the app and go to Settings > Home Screen Widgets.
  2. Tap Add the Today widget or Add the Add a task bar, and confirm on your home screen.
  3. If your phone doesn't offer that, press and hold an empty part of your home screen, choose Widgets, then find Eisenhower Matrix.

Why a widget fits the Eisenhower method

  • Urgent tasks stay visible. Late and due-today tasks are exactly what slips when the app is closed.
  • Capture gets faster. One tap from the home screen means fewer tasks held in your head.
  • Important tasks get dates. Once a Schedule task has a due date, it turns up on the widget on the day, instead of hiding in a box you rarely open.

Tips

  • Put the Today widget on your first home screen page, not the third.
  • Keep it short: if it shows more than five tasks, run a quick overdue clear-out.
  • Pair the widget with the free Daily Review once a day, so the widget shows a list you have actually decided on.
